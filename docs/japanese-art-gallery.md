# Japanese public-domain gallery

The slideshow uses five public-domain Japanese woodblock prints, converted to
600×400 pixels and the e-paper module's black, white, yellow, red, blue, and
green palette.

- [The Great Wave off the Coast of Kanagawa — Katsushika Hokusai](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Katsushika_Hokusai_-_The_Great_Wave_off_the_Coast_of_Kanagawa_LCCN2008660568.jpg)
- [Fine Wind, Clear Morning / Red Fuji — Katsushika Hokusai](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Fine_Wind,_Clear_Morning_by_Hokusai_(Shimane_Art_Museum).jpg)
- [Plum Garden, Kameido — Ando Hiroshige](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ando_Hiroshige_-_Plum_Garden,_Kameido_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg)
- [Sudden Shower over Shin-Ohashi Bridge and Atake — Utagawa Hiroshige](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Hiroshige,_Sudden_shower_over_Shin-Ohashi_bridge_and_Atake,_1857_expanded.jpg)
- [Snow in Kanbara — Utagawa Hiroshige](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:HIROSHIGE-Snow-Kanbara-Tokaido.jpg)

The firmware shows one image, sleeps the panel, waits three minutes, and then
refreshes the next image.
